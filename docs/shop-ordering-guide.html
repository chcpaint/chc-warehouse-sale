Ordering with CHC

Your online ordering page — how to get in, find what you need, raise a quote for approval, and turn it into an order. Five minutes to read, then you have it.

Getting in

Your page
chcsale.com/store/your-shop
Access code
supplied by CHC
One code for the whole shop. There are no individual usernames or passwords to hand out or reset. Anyone at the counter can order; whoever places it puts their own name and email on the order.

Finding what you need

The prices you see are yours. Your catalogue holds your own part numbers at your own agreed prices — it is not a general price list.

Placing an order

1

Build the cart, download the quote

Add what you need, open the Cart, and choose Download Quote (PDF). Send that to whoever approves spending and gets you a PO number.

Nothing is ordered at this point. A quote is just a quote — no email goes to CHC and no stock is committed.
2

Convert it to an order

Back in the Cart, choose Convert to Order, enter the PO number from your approved quote, confirm who is ordering and which location it is for, then Submit Order.

Now it is live. CHC and your own contacts are emailed immediately, and your branch begins picking it.

If CHC issues your PO numbers rather than your accounts team, one is allocated automatically and shown on your confirmation. If your shop does not use POs at all, the step is simply not there.

What happens next

You are emailed at every stage. Nobody has to ring to find out where an order is.

EmailMeans
Order receivedCHC has it. Nothing further needed from you.
Invoice readyYour branch has invoiced it. Download the invoice from Order History.
Payment receivedSettled and closed.

Every past order stays under Order History with its PO number, what was on it, and the invoice to download whenever you need it again.

Worth knowing

“Contact for current pricing” Order it exactly as normal. CHC confirms the price when your branch invoices — it is priced at the true cost on the day.
Multiple locations Choose the location at sign-in. It decides which CHC branch serves the order, so pick the right one.
Something missing from your list? Ask CHC. If a part exists it can be added to your catalogue at your price, usually the same day.
Wrong price or wrong pack size? Tell CHC before you order. Do not work around it.
Questions about your catalogue, prices or access code — speak to your CHC representative.
