Order Desk Handbook

How to sign in, work an order through to payment, and know exactly what the shop is told at each point. Markham and Woodbridge counters, and head office.

Signing in

Address
chcsale.com/admin
Username
your @chcpaint.com email
First password
CHCsaleportal
You will be asked to choose your own password the first time. Everyone starts on the same one, so until you replace it the system cannot tell you apart from anyone else at your branch — and the order history would name the wrong person. Nothing else opens until you do it. Eight characters or more, with a capital, a lowercase and a number.

After that you land straight on the Orders screen. There is nothing else to click — no dashboard, no product lists, no company settings. That is deliberate: your account only reaches order management, so there is nothing here you can break.

Orders can be grouped by branch, and every order shows a Handled by column — whoever last uploaded an invoice, changed a status or closed it. If a shop rings asking who dealt with their order, that column is the answer.

Working an order

1

The order arrives Needs Invoice

The shop places it themselves through their store page. It appears on your screen in amber with the shop, the location, the contact and the total. Nothing is required from you to receive it.

Email sent automatically: “Order received”, to the person who ordered, the shop's own contacts and managers, and your branch. This is the email that tells you an order is waiting.
2

Upload the invoice Awaiting Payment

Pick and pack as normal, raise the invoice in your usual system, then:

  1. Find the order — the amber Upload Invoice button is on its row.
  2. Choose the invoice file. PDF, PNG or JPG.
  3. The row turns blue and reads Awaiting Payment.
Email sent automatically: “Invoice ready”, to the same people. The shop does not need chasing — they are told the moment you upload.

Uploaded the wrong file? Use the circular arrow on the row to replace it. The shop is notified again.

3

Mark paid and close Paid & Closed

When payment lands, press Mark Paid & Close. The order turns green and drops out of your working list.

Email sent automatically: “Payment received”, to the same people.

One thing to watch

The status dropdown does not email anyone.

The small dropdown on each row — confirmed, processing, shipped, delivered — is for your own notes. Setting it to shipped tells the shop nothing.

Only the three steps above send email. If you want the shop to know something, it has to be one of those, or a phone call.
