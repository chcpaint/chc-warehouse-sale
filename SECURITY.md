# Security policy

refinishAI (the console behind chcsale.com) is used by collision shops and their
distributors as a system of record, so security reports are handled first.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **adam@chcpaint.com** with the subject "Security report": what you found,
where, and how to reproduce it. Please do not open a public GitHub issue.

- We acknowledge within two business days and keep you updated until it is fixed.
- Confirmed issues are fixed as a priority; critical ones the same day where possible.
- Good-faith research is welcome. Please do not access, change or keep other
  people's data, do not degrade the service (no load or denial-of-service
  testing), and give us reasonable time to fix before telling others.

Machine-readable contact: https://chcsale.com/.well-known/security.txt

## What is in place

See the Help page (https://chcsale.com/help#security) and `docs/OPERATIONS.md`
(backups, restore drills, monitoring) and `docs/licensing.md` (separation
between distributors).
